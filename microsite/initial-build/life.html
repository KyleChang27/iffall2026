Life & legacy

Brooklyn beginnings. Downtown experiments. A lasting visual language.

1960–1977
Beginnings

Born in Brooklyn to a Haitian father and Puerto Rican mother, Basquiat grew up in New York. Drawing and an interest in anatomy would become part of his artistic vocabulary.

1978–1980
Downtown

Working under the name SAMO with Al Diaz, he brought cryptic phrases into the city's streets. His early practice also included music with the band Gray.

1981–1986
Recognition

Gallery exhibitions brought international attention. Painting, drawing, language, and collaboration became central to his career, including his work with Andy Warhol.

1988 & beyond
Legacy

Basquiat died in New York in 1988, aged 27. His work continues to shape conversations about race, identity, power, and the boundaries between popular culture and fine art.
